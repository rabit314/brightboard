Tips & Features

  • Open Shapes in the toolbox for lines, rectangles, circles and arrows. Use the color dropdown for more shades, or the rainbow picker for any color.
  • Group multiple selected items to select and transform them together. Ungroup before editing individual members. Grouping existing groups combines them into one group.
  • Lock a selection to protect it from moving, resizing, rotating, erasing and property edits. Click a locked item and use the lock button to unlock it. Clear page still clears everything.
  • Hold Shift while drawing shapes for perfect squares, circles and 15° snapped lines.
  • With Select tool, double-click text or sticky notes to edit; double-click empty canvas to create text.
  • Rotate drawings & objects — drag the dedicated rotation handle blob above the selection (hold Shift to snap).
  • Every object resizes — drag any corner handle of the selection bounding box.
  • Ctrl/⌘ + scroll zooms · plain scroll pans · right-drag pans · pinch with 2 fingers on touch.
  • Scroll over stroke or font tabs with mouse wheel to quickly adjust size (Shift = 5× step).
  • Your notebook autosaves in this browser and restores on reload (Ctrl+S saves immediately).
  • Move your mouse near the left side for tools or near the bottom for pages. Use each panel's pin toggle to keep it visible. Touch controls stay visible.
  • The pen and highlighter cursor previews your brush size at the current zoom.
  • Paint Bucket (B) fills the clicked connected colour region on the visible canvas, including closed outlines. Grid lines are ignored; Ctrl+Z undoes a fill.
